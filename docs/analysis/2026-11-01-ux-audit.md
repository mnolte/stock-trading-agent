# UX audit of the design

By Claude, 1 November 2026, on every screen in `design/screens/` at 1440 px and 390 px. The question for each screen was: can Marc see quickly what needs him, what the agent did and why, and how it is doing? The three biggest problems were fixed in the same change (PR 35 in the build plan); the rest are listed in order of value.

## Fixed in this change

1. **Nothing collects what needs Marc** (high).
   - The problems are spread across separate places:
     - banners under the header (only the ones with a banner);
     - unreviewed errors on Readiness;
     - verdicts to confirm on Reviews;
     - approvals in the feed;
     - a failing email channel nowhere at all.
   - On 30 October two cycles ran without model answers and only a per-symbol warning went out.
   - Now: a **Needs you** card first on the dashboard, a count in the header on every screen, and a health line (Alpaca sync, order stream, clock, keys, email).
2. **Open positions sat at the bottom of the dashboard** (high).
   - They came below a ten-row watchlist, and nothing showed how close a position is to its stop.
   - Now they sit under the cycle strip next to the risk budget, each with a stop-to-target bar and the distance to the stop in ATR (amber under 1.5).
   - The duplicate "Positions 2 of 4" tile is gone.
   - On **Positions**:
     - a totals line ("if every stop hits −$32.80");
     - four facts per position (result at the stop, to the stop, to the target, planned exit);
     - a link to the latest decision;
     - the Alpaca legs folded into one line that opens by itself on a problem.
3. **Phone width** (high).
   - Every screen started with the full 930 px sidebar before any content; the app already has a menu below 820 px, so the design lagged it.
   - Settings scrolled sideways at 390 px: 481 px wide, because of the recovery-codes row.
   - The new position rows also overflowed while they were being designed.
   - Now: a sticky top bar (title, Needs you count, menu), no sideways scroll on any app screen.

## Next, in order of value

4. **Decision drawers bury the outcome** (medium).
   - The drawers are 1,600–2,800 px long. What finally happened (code changed the action, warnings, gates that failed) sits in the gate list near the bottom, under the options and levels.
   - Proposal: a "What happened" block under the action, with the final action, every change by code with its reason, and the warnings. Then fold Levels by default.
5. **Four drawer layouts** (medium). v1–v4 order their sections differently. One order for all: action, what happened, checks, options, reasoning, gates, levels, outcome.
6. **The briefing card repeats the cards around it** (medium). Positions, regime and banners are already on the dashboard. Shorter: the text and "numbers checked", with the fact chips only on hover.
7. **Watchlist at scale** (medium). In screened mode it lists about 47 symbols. Default the filter to "Pass rules + Held" and keep "All" one click away.
8. **Settings is 10,465 px on one page** (medium). A sticky section list and a "Changed from default" filter would make it scannable.
9. **Performance** (low).
   - The 4 + 2 KPI tiles leave a gap.
   - The equity chart's axis labels are odd values ($10,181, $10,109); round ticks read faster.
10. **Order history** (low).
    - A rejected order should be red, while "not filled" stays neutral.
    - A "problems only" filter would help.
11. **Leftovers** (low).
    - `Inspector-error.png` and the decision drawer PNGs were not re-rendered. Only the shared CSS changed for the drawers, so they look the same.
    - The States reference sheet scrolls sideways at 390 px; it is a reference, not a screen.

## What works well

- The cycle strip with its countdown and dots.
- The risk budget meters.
- The decision feed grouped per cycle.
- The Readiness page: one verdict, tiles, and each criterion with its target and status.
- The calm colour use: amber and red only where something is wrong.
