Off-hours the card shows the most recent decision for the symbol, with its date.

Outside a session, “today” means the last session: the feed is titled with its date (“Friday 23 Oct”), the model-call tile counts that session, and the latest-decision card falls back to the most recent decision with its date. A countdown longer than 24 hours is replaced by the day and time.
