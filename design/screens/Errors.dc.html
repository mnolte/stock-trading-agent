Error pages

Two kinds. In-app pages are drawn by the frontend inside the normal sidebar and header. Static pages are plain HTML files served by Caddy when the app itself can't answer: they load nothing from the app and work without JavaScript.

In the app

Not found · a record
A decision, order, call, run or strategy id that doesn't exist
stock.example.nl/decisions/98213
PAPER
404 · not found

Decision 98213 doesn't exist

It may be from a run that was reset, or the link is mistyped. Decisions are never deleted within a run.

Something went wrong on this page

The page hit an error and stopped drawing. The agent itself is not affected and keeps trading. The error was logged with the id above.

Only this screen failed; the sidebar and the other screens still work.

Stock Trading Agent isn't responding

Open positions keep their stops and targets at Alpaca, whatever this page shows.

If this lasts more than a few minutes, the outside heartbeat check emails you. Once the app is back, the Logs screen shows what happened.
